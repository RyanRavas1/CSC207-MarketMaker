## What is not tested, and why

### `com.marketmaker`, `Main`, 4%

`Main` is the composition root. Its body is object construction and wiring: it reads
environment variables, chooses a `PriceFeed` implementation, builds every interactor,
presenter and controller, and shows the frame. There is no branching logic to assert on
that would not simply restate the wiring, and exercising it means launching a Swing
window and hitting the network.

We excluded it on purpose. The behaviour it wires up is covered at the layer below:
`createPriceFeed`'s two branches are covered by the `FinnhubPriceFeed` and
`ReplayPriceFeed` tests, and `createHistoricalData`'s missing-key fallback is covered by
the historical data-access tests.

### `view`, Swing panels, 65%

Swing rendering is not unit tested. Asserting on pixels or on component trees is brittle,
and it tests the toolkit more than it tests our code. What we do cover here is the
non-visual logic that leaked into the package and is worth protecting: table models and
cell rendering decisions.

The panels are verified manually against the demo script in `docs/local/demo-script.md`.

### `interface_adapter`, controllers and presenters, 53%

This is the weakest number in the project and the one we would fix first with more time.

The package splits cleanly in two:

- **Tested (100%):** `ViewModel`, `OrderTicketController`, and five presenters
  (`CandlestickChartPresenter`, `OrderHistoryPresenter`, `PositionsPresenter`,
  `PortfolioSummaryPresenter`, `WatchlistPresenter` at 93%), plus `Format` at 88%.
  `OrderTicketController` is the one controller with real logic. It parses free text from
  the order ticket and rejects bad input before anything is dispatched, and it is fully
  covered, including every rejection path.
- **Untested (0%):** `WatchlistController`, `ChartController`, `RealizedPnLController`,
  `ProfileController`, `CancelOrderController`, and four presenters
  (`OrderFeedbackPresenter`, `TickerSearchPresenter`, `RealizedPnLPresenter`,
  `ProfilePresenter`).

Every class in the untested group is thin. The controllers normalise a string, guard
against empty input, and hand a request model to the worker executor. The presenters
format a message and write it to a `ViewModel`. Neither group makes a decision that could
be wrong in an interesting way, and the pattern each follows is already proven by
`OrderTicketControllerTest` and the `ViewModel` tests.

We are not claiming this is ideal. It is a real gap, not a principled exclusion. The
honest reason is that these classes were written late, and testing effort went to the
interactors where the business rules live.

## What we would do next

1. Cover the nine 0% adapters. They are thin enough that a fake interactor and a captured
   `ViewModel` state would take one short test each.
2. Extract the remaining logic out of `view` so the package is purely rendering, which
   would make its 65% an accurate reflection of "only Swing is untested".
3. Add failure-path tests for `CancelOrderInteractor` and `ViewCandlestickChartInteractor`
   to bring the interactor figure to 100%.
